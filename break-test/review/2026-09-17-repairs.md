# September 17 break-test expansion and repairs

All work extends the existing `break-test` workspace package. No production
dependencies or persisted feature schemas were added. Existing uncommitted
work was preserved.

correct material.

## Defects and corrections

1. **Translated joins rejected.** Infinite plane-intersection lines were
   artificially restricted to parameters −100 through +100. Real face
   intersections outside that interval were omitted. Bounds now come from
   projecting the operands' face bounding boxes, and nearest-point projection
   onto lines is analytic. This fixes the reproduced translated box fuses and
   also protects cut/common. Body Join preserves the kernel failure reason
   instead of always claiming that the bodies do not touch.
   The original search envelope remains a minimum, with a tolerance margin
   around projected bounds, to preserve intersections near trimmed endpoints.
2. **Expression process abort.** Ten thousand nested parentheses reproducibly
   overflowed a test worker's stack. Tokenization now limits input to 64 KiB,
   parsing limits parenthesis depth to 64, and unary signs are iterative.
   This is a denial-of-service robustness issue in user/document expressions;
   no code-execution vulnerability was demonstrated.
3. **Scale creates non-finite display data.** Staged scale geometry is checked
   before the source body is consumed. Non-finite display output is rejected
   with a feature-attributed warning and full source preservation.
4. **Coincident loft creates a phantom body.** Coplanar sections are rejected
   before skinning with a diagnostic, rather than exposing a body with no
   enclosed volume.
5. **Closed polygonal sweep loses material.** Bisector sections lacked the
   miter stretch needed to preserve leg cross-section; a square lost about
   29% of its volume. Closed-path tangents now bisect unit directions, miter
   sections compensate for the bend, and the seam duplicates the first exact
   section. Guided sweeps retain their separate implementation.
6. **Overlapping cuts partially commit.** Collapsed circular tools lost their
   analytic profile provenance, preventing a single sectional cut. That
   provenance is retained; primitive quadrilateral profiles can recover exact
   line spans. A failed multi-tool or multi-body cut rolls back the whole
   feature, including any earlier successful tools.
7. **Shell retains stale construction geometry.** Successful hollowing now
   invalidates the original filled-prism provenance. Later sectional cuts and
   joins cannot reconstruct that filled prism and silently refill the cavity.
8. **Generated join faces cannot be edited.** Sectional join results now assign
   durable names, owning bodies and producing features to newly generated
   faces, while preserving matched input names. The join lifecycle tests check
   every face's identity after evaluation, persistence and depth changes.
9. **Identical rebuilds change triangulation.** Analytic and sampled profile
   boundary merging iterated randomly seeded hash maps. Ordered boundary maps
   stabilize loop starts and triangulation. The overhang regression repeats
   independent cold builds eight times without relaxing bit-identical volume.
10. **Counterbores through joined bosses fail.** Cylindrical hole tools now
    retain analytic profiles. Sectional cuts accept unions of parallel prisms
    and retain the resulting sections for subsequent operations. The six-boss
    cylinder-head regression now requires every feature to resolve, and the
    three new construction-plane tests require correct remaining material.
11. **Sectional bore faces cannot be healed.** Generated cut walls now receive
    editing identities. Cylinder material-side classification uses the closed
    mesh's outward normals instead of trusting only a sewn face's orientation
    flag. Exact healing supports a through-bore bounded by two circular cap
    openings, avoiding a coincident-cylinder fuse. Six plane/direction variants
    check the restored plate, including independent cold evaluation.
12. **Sectioned solids have incorrect native orientation.** A dedicated prism
    section path aligns curved loops with their intrinsic surface normals
    before final sewing establishes the material side. The existing blend
    builder retains its separate orientation contract. Counterbore regressions
    check native tessellated volume as well as display volume. Line-only region
    cuts retain their polygonal builder.
13. **Repeated holes lose inherited face names.** Multiple source patches on
    one supporting surface may match only when their durable names agree.
    Tool patches can share their generated identity without erasing earlier
    hole identities.
14. **A near-miss cut removes material.** Recovery variants are attempted only
    when the requested tool bounds overlap the part with positive thickness
    on every axis. A grown recovery tool cannot turn a missed cut into a hit.
15. **A bore cannot be selected for threading.** Exact boolean history now
    records the result face's supporting surface kind and retains inherited
    producer identity. Selection no longer depends on a curved face averaging
    to an exactly zero display normal. The bolt-in-tapped-plate regression
    passes after this repair.
16. **Merging reverse-direction edges corrupts face loops.** The kernel arena
    deduplicated geometrically identical edges but did not reverse their
    coedge uses and pcurve ranges when their natural directions differed.
    This could invert entire bosses after sewing. The merge now preserves
    traversal and parametric direction; a low-level regression covers both
    input orders and the joined-counterbore tests check the resulting solids.
17. **Historical patterns rebuild from the wrong checkpoint.** Recoverable
    rectangular source profiles are accepted alongside retained analytic
    profiles when selecting a reusable checkpoint. Exact sectional subtraction
    recovers source material deltas without a coincident-shell boolean where
    both operands retain their source profiles. The damaged-checkpoint fixture
    explicitly orders its witness before the pattern.
18. **Simple circular cuts change downstream blend meshes.** Untouched boxes
    with one smooth cutting tool retain their established native boolean
    path. Compound profiles and accumulated joins/cuts use sectional rebuilds.
    The existing cut/fillet order-equivalence and trimmed-cylinder quality
    checks pass without changing their tolerances.
19. **Very deep holes are mistaken for misses.** Contact tolerances now use
    the intersection interval and smaller operand's extent, not a cutter's
    remote end. The local extent retains cast uncertainty at zero-coordinate
    contact so rotated face sketches still select the inward cut direction.
    The existing blind/exact-through/deeper/through-all comparison includes a
    billion-millimeter depth and passes with its original volume tolerances.

Derived kernel and mesh cache ABIs are incremented to 11 so reopening an old
document rebuilds corrected geometry without changing its authoritative recipe.

## Honest rejection and corrected test oracles

- Exactly tangent through-bores leave a zero-thickness wall. The active test
  requires complete rollback plus an attributed warning. It does not claim
  that this non-manifold result is now a supported solid.
- Tangential movement of a planar box face is a real shear that preserves
  volume. Its regression now checks changed occupancy rather than treating
  unchanged volume as proof of failure.
- The old overlapping-circle area formula subtracted the overlap lens twice.
  The analytic oracle now uses the standard single-lens subtraction.
- The whole-sketch overlapping-bore characterization now requires the complete
  union rather than freezing the historical missing bore.
- Crossing-rib characterizations now require both ribs and the plate, their
  exact union volume, occupancy, watertightness, and one connected solid.
- The cylinder-head oracle now squares the drill radius and counts only the
  central bore's 12 mm intersection with material, excluding 10 mm of air.
  Its old 5% whole-body tolerance is tightened to 0.5% plus warning checks.
- Previously ignored catalog regressions are enabled. Safe rejection remains
  an explicitly tested outcome only where the operation is unsupported.
